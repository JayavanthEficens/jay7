    Sustainable Computing

    We understand that many private and public sector organizations need help achieving their sustainability goals or reducing operational costs. Whether in data centers or machinery, Eficens supports and produces technologies in clean energy equipment and intelligent monitoring and metering that tracks usage and savings and provides predictive analytics relevant to ES&G goals.

    Furthermore, Eficens has robust cloud solutions capabilities and is an expert at cloud migration for its clients. A cloud hosting provider can better deploy IT infrastructure with greater efficiency at scale - and far more efficiently than IT operated in client-server environments. This results in substantial energy savings and decreased waste because the amount of equipment required per user in the cloud is far less.
